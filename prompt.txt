Create a complete Odoo 17 Community Edition custom module for managing Algerian public procurement procedures (Consultations, Tenders, Competitions) compliant with Law n°23-12 of August 5, 2023, and Presidential Decree n°15-247.

MODULE REQUIREMENTS:

The system must automatically determine procurement method based on:
- Estimated amount including tax (DZD)
- Service type (Works/Supplies vs Studies/Services)
- Structure level (Central vs Regional)

The system must automatically determine the procurement method based on:

CENTRAL LEVEL - WORKS & SUPPLIES:
- Below 50,000 DZD: Direct Purchase Order / Service Order
- 50,000 to below 1,000,000: Comparative Offer Table (COT)
- 1,000,000 to 12,000,000: Consultation
- 12,000,000 to 30,000,000: Tender
- Above 30,000,000: Tender

CENTRAL LEVEL - STUDIES & SERVICES:
- Below 50,000 DZD: Direct Purchase Order / Service Order
- 50,000 to below 500,000: Comparative Offer Table (COT)
- 500,000 to 6,000,000: Consultation
- 6,000,000 to 15,000,000: Tender
- Above 15,000,000: Tender

REGIONAL LEVEL - WORKS & SUPPLIES:
- Below 50,000 DZD: Direct Purchase Order / Service Order
- 50,000 to below 1,000,000: Comparative Offer Table (COT)
- 1,000,000 and above: Consultation

REGIONAL LEVEL - STUDIES & SERVICES:
- Below 50,000 DZD: Direct Purchase Order / Service Order
- 50,000 to below 500,000: Comparative Offer Table (COT)
- 500,000 and above: Consultation
